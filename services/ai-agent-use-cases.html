AI Agent Use Cases

Where agents create value — by workflow, not by technology.

A look at the kinds of business processes InsightnWisdom's agentic AI practice is built to support, and the operating model behind every one of them.

Where agents create value

Six workflow patterns we build against

Each use case below is a starting pattern, not a fixed product — the agent's scope, data access and approval points are defined per engagement.

Knowledge & RAG Assistants

Grounded assistants that answer questions from approved internal documents, policies and knowledge bases — with citations back to source.

IT Operations Support

Agents that triage alerts, enrich tickets with context, and route incidents to the right team inside your ITSM tool.

Customer & Service Operations

Front-line assistants that handle routine inquiries and escalate exceptions to a human, with full conversation context preserved.

Sales & Proposal Support

Agents that assemble first-draft proposals, pull relevant case history, and flag pricing or scope items for review.

Finance & Procurement Workflows

Agents that reconcile line items, flag exceptions against policy, and prepare approval packets for a human sign-off.

Field & Maintenance Operations

Agents that draft service reports, cross-check parts against inventory, and prepare escalation notes for engineers.

How it works

Enterprise data + applications + AI agents + human approvals

An agent's usefulness comes from what it's connected to and what it's allowed to do — not from the model alone. Every deployment defines these four layers explicitly before go-live.

Enterprise data

Approved documents, records and knowledge repositories.

Applications

CRM, ERP, ITSM, collaboration and cloud systems.

AI agents

Role-scoped agents that plan and execute defined tasks.

Human approvals

Checkpoints at the decisions that carry real consequence.

Governance

Security, permissions, auditability, evaluation and human oversight are part of the design — not an afterthought.

  • Identity and access scoped to the minimum the agent needs
  • Every action logged and auditable
  • Evaluation before and after production release
  • A named human owner for each agent's decisions
