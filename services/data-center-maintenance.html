IT & Data Center Maintenance

Health checks, break/fix, and lifecycle support for servers and network infrastructure.

Server and network health checks, diagnostics, break/fix, replacement-part recommendation, installation, commissioning and lifecycle support.

The situation

Hardware doesn't fail on a schedule.

Racks, cabling and network equipment need routine health checks and a fast, competent response when something breaks — not just a warranty number to call.

Our approach

Field engineering, close to the equipment.

Our engineers work on-site and remote across Seoul and Hong Kong: diagnosing, replacing, installing and commissioning — including structured cabling and rack-and-stack work.
