Managed IT Services

Monitoring, incident handling and escalation — run to a defined model.

Monitoring, incident handling, technical follow-up, escalation, remote/on-site support, reporting and SLA-based operations.

The situation

Problems found late cost more than problems found early.

Without continuous monitoring and a clear escalation path, small issues in servers and network equipment turn into outages.

Our approach

A defined coverage and escalation model.

We monitor, triage, and escalate against a model agreed with you — and report on what happened, not just that something did.
